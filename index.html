About AnchorDrop

AnchorDrop is a platform designed to make discovering and reserving docking spaces easier for boaters. We connect boat owners with private dock owners and marina operators, helping them find suitable docking spaces with greater convenience. Our goal is to make every docking experience simpler, more transparent and more reliable.

Terms & Conditions

Using AnchorDrop, you agree to use the platform responsibly and provide accurate information.

  • Users must provide correct registration and booking details.
  • Dock owners are responsible for maintaining accurate listing information, including dimensions, facilities, availability and pricing.
  • Boaters must verify docking suitability and confirm local conditions before arrival.
  • Booking requests and cancellations are subject to the policies displayed for each listing.
  • AnchorDrop is a platform connecting boaters and dock providers; it does not guarantee the safety or suitability of any docking location

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. AnchorDrop may collect information necessary to provide its services, including:

We aim to use this information only for providing and improving our services. Personal information should not be shared with third parties without an appropriate purpose or legal basis.
